Shadow

Set aside, for now.

For a few days this page held a tool. It read the export a platform hands you, entirely in your browser, and put a dollar figure on what your data had earned the company that held it. A second page projected a dividend from the same figures. Both have been taken down. The work is kept, unpublished, on a branch of the site's repository.

Why

The idea is intriguing and we still hold it: a person's digital shadow has a value, and a share of that value is owed back to the person who casts it. But the number we were computing was not that value. It was a projection from today's technology companies — their revenue per user, their margins, held flat across a lifetime. That measures one use of a shadow, by one kind of company, under exactly the arrangement we are arguing against.

The value of a shadow held by its owner, pooled with others on terms they set, will be greatly amplified by the AI that results from it. Nothing in an annual report measures that, and no arithmetic on those reports reaches it. Estimating it well requires more work than projecting numbers from today's companies. We would rather publish nothing than a figure that is precise and beside the point.

What remains

Where the export button is still tells you how to get your own copy of what each platform holds. The terms, read still quotes what they say they keep. When there is a method that deserves this page, it will come back here.
