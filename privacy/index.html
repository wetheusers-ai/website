Privacy

What we keep, and how to leave.

When you add your voice at the call, we store what you gave us: your name if you gave one, your email if you gave one, the community you named, the time you joined, and a salted hash of your IP address — not the address itself — used only to slow down abuse of the form. That is the whole record.

Who touches it

Three companies, all under contract, none permitted to use it for anything but running this: Cloudflare stores the record and serves the page, Resend sends the one confirmation email, and Mailgun carries mail addressed to hello@wetheusers.ai — including, if you use it, your request to be deleted. Each of those three runs on infrastructure of its own, listed on its own site. We add no one beyond them.

What we never do

  • We never sell it, and we never share it beyond the three companies named above.
  • We never train a model on it.
  • We never show your name publicly. What is public is a count — how many confirmed voices a community has — never a list of who they are.

How to leave

Every confirmation email carries a link that deletes your row, permanently. If you never received or kept that email and want your record found and removed, write to hello@wetheusers.ai and we'll do it by hand.

If you joined without giving an email, there is no link to send you and no way for us to prove a request is yours. What we hold in that case is a first name, a community, and a timestamp. Write to hello@wetheusers.ai, tell us what you entered and roughly when, and we will find it and remove it.

The one thing we watch

We count page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics. We do not know who you are.
